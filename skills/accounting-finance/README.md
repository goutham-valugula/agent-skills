# Accounting & Finance Skills

Practical AI workflows for small-business bookkeeping review. Choose a single skill folder and give its `SKILL.md` to an assistant that supports Agent Skills.

## Available

### [Bank reconciliation reviewer](bank-reconciliation-reviewer/SKILL.md)

Compare a user-provided bank export with a ledger or bookkeeping export. It suggests matches only when the evidence supports them, separates ambiguous items for a person to check, and does not post entries or change the source files.

**Useful for:** small-business owners, bookkeepers, and finance teams who need a reviewable first pass over exported transaction files.

**Not for:** connecting to bank accounts, replacing accounting software, giving tax advice, or approving an official reconciliation.

## Use only what you need

1. Open the individual skill folder above and review its `SKILL.md`.
2. Provide only the relevant statement and ledger exports; remove full account numbers and unrelated private transactions first.
3. Follow your AI assistant's current instructions for adding or supplying a skill. Platform support and file handling vary.
4. Verify every proposed match and exception against the original records before changing your books.

Want a different accounting workflow? Suggest the specific task and what a good result would look like. New skills are added when a focused, useful, and safe workflow is clear.
